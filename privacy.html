Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CyberGame Limited ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in our applications and games that link to this policy ("Apps"), including Jewel Muse!, on iOS, iPadOS, Android, the Apple App Store, and Google Play. Features, permissions, and service providers may vary by App, platform, version, and region. If an App provides a separate privacy policy, that policy applies to that App.

You can contact us about privacy or request deletion of your information at x@cybergame.ai. This policy describes our practices; it does not replace any consent or permission required by law or your device.

1. Information We Handle

Jewel Muse! does not require a separate CyberGame account to play. However, our Apps and their service providers can process personal information, including online identifiers, even when you do not register an account.

  • Device and connection information: device model, operating system and App version, language, time zone, network information, IP address, and approximate location inferred from an IP address. Advertising and measurement services may process the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA, subject to tracking permission), Android advertising ID, App instance or installation identifiers, and SDK-generated identifiers such as Adjust ADID.
  • Gameplay and usage information: sessions, level starts and completions, progress, play time, use of boosters and rewards, daily challenges, streaks, co-op events, settings interactions, and experiment assignments. Notification permission responses and reminder interactions may also be recorded.
  • Advertising and attribution information: ad requests, impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad outcomes, ad placement and network, impression revenue, installation and referral information, campaign information, and deep-link interactions. These help measure which campaigns bring players to an App and how ads perform.
  • Diagnostics: errors, crash or performance information, and ad-delivery or ad-quality diagnostics generated by the App, platform, or integrated services.
  • Saved game and co-op information: game progress, inventories, unfinished boards, challenge results, streak history, and co-op snapshots. Supported co-op features exchange pairing/session identifiers, connection information, and shared game state with another player's device.
  • Information you provide: if you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. Please avoid sending information that is not needed for your request.
  • Privacy preferences: consent decisions, tracking authorization status, and related preference signals used to apply your choices.

Jewel Muse! does not need access to your contacts or precise GPS location for its gameplay. Approximate location used by advertising services is different from GPS access. Optional camera and local-network features are described below.

2. Why We Use Information

  • Operate the Apps, save and restore progress, provide co-op play, and deliver rewards and reminders.
  • Understand gameplay, improve stability, balance difficulty, and evaluate features through remote configuration and A/B experiments.
  • Deliver and measure advertising, measure installation campaigns and ad revenue, and detect invalid traffic or other abuse.
  • Apply privacy preferences, respond to support and privacy requests, and meet legal obligations.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for processing that requires it, providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in maintaining and improving the Apps and preventing abuse where permitted, or compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent as described in Section 4.

4. Advertising, Consent, and Your Choices

Apps with advertising may display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Where required, a consent-management flow presents choices about advertising and measurement. In supported versions and regions, open the App's Settings and select Privacy Options to review or change your choices. You can also contact us using Section 10.

  • iOS / iPadOS: Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission controls access to IDFA and tracking subject to Apple's rules. You can change this under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Refusing tracking does not necessarily stop contextual ads, App analytics, or privacy-preserving attribution.
  • Android: use your device's Google / Privacy / Ads settings to reset or delete your advertising ID and manage available ad-personalization controls. Names and availability vary by Android version and device manufacturer.
  • Consent and opt-out: where applicable, use the in-App privacy choices to withdraw consent or opt out of personalized advertising or sale/sharing for targeted advertising. Device advertising-ID controls and in-App consent choices serve different purposes; you may need to use both.

Changing your choices affects future processing subject to applicable law. It does not automatically delete previously collected information. Non-personalized ads can still involve limited information for delivery, frequency management, measurement, and fraud prevention.

5. Storage, Sync, and Optional Permissions

Local storage and cloud saves. Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Supported Apple versions also use iCloud / CloudKit to synchronize game progress, inventories, snapshots, daily challenges, and streaks through your Apple account when iCloud is available. This is an Apple-platform feature, not a promise of Android-to-iOS save synchronization. Platform backup settings may also preserve App data.

Server processing. Analytics, attribution, advertising, and remote-configuration services send relevant data to their servers. Local game storage does not mean that all App information stays on your device.

Co-op and camera. Where available, local-network or nearby-device access connects players for co-op play, and camera access lets you scan a pairing QR code. Pairing and game-state information is exchanged to run the shared game. Permission availability depends on the platform and feature.

Notifications and Live Activities. Supported features use game activity and progress to schedule local reminders and display streak information in notifications, widgets, or Live Activities. Reminder interactions may be measured as usage events. You can manage notifications and available Live Activity controls in system settings.

You can deny or revoke optional permissions in your device settings. The related feature may then be unavailable, while other features can continue to work.

6. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described here, including providing the service, resolving support requests, maintaining security, and meeting legal obligations. The period depends on the type of information, the service involved, and the reason it is retained. Service providers have their own retention practices described in their notices. Information we no longer need is deleted or de-identified, subject to applicable legal requirements and backup processes.

  • Device data: delete the App to remove its local data; on Android, you can also clear its storage in system settings. Offloading an App, deleting a shortcut, or reinstalling from a backup may leave or restore data.
  • Cloud saves and backups: deleting the App does not necessarily delete iCloud saves or device backups. Use the relevant Apple or Google account storage/backup controls where available, or contact us for help identifying the appropriate deletion steps.
  • Server and SDK data: email x@cybergame.ai with the App name, platform, and your request. If available, include an in-App player identifier so we can locate the relevant records. We may request limited additional information to verify and fulfill your request; do not send passwords. We will handle information under our control and coordinate with relevant providers where appropriate. Providers acting independently may also offer direct deletion tools in their privacy policies.

You do not need a CyberGame account to submit a privacy request. Uninstalling an App or resetting an advertising ID does not itself delete information already held by us or our providers.
